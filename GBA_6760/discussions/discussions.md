## Week 1 – Cloud Value Proposition

**Question:**  
Cloud computing and cloud services have many value propositions. Please select one proposition and provide arguments to justify why this proposition is the most important. (200-300 words)

**Response:**
Cloud computing offers many value propositions, but I believe the most important is operational resilience. Operational resilience is an organization’s ability to continue operating during hardware failures, cyber incidents, natural disasters, or sudden increases in demand. As discussed in Chapter 1 of the Cloud Security Handbook, traditional on prem data centers are limited by physical hardware, minimal redundancy, and high maintenance overhead. These constraints make outages harder to prevent and slower to recover from. Public cloud providers address these challenges by building infrastructure that is designed to recover from failure rather than avoid it entirely Operational resilience is supported by core cloud characteristics defined by NIST, including resource pooling, broad network access, and rapid elasticity.

AWS identifies operational resilience as a key part of its Cloud Value Framework and notes that downtime can cost organizations hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars per hour. While cloud computing also improves cost efficiency and agility, those benefits depend on systems being available. Even the ability to scale quickly would not matter if cloud providers could not meet uptime guarantees through strong SLAs. Without it, the other advantages of cloud computing lose their value, making operational resilience the most important cloud value proposition.
